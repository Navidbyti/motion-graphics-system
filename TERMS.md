# Terms of use

By installing or using Motion Graphics, you agree to what follows. If you do not
agree, do not use the software.

## The software is free

You may use Motion Graphics for personal or commercial work at no cost. You may
install it on as many machines as you like. There is no licence fee, no seat
count, and no expiry.

It is provided as-is, with no warranty. Renders, exports and transcriptions are
produced on your own machine and are your responsibility to check before you
publish them.

## Templates you create may be included in the software

If you create a template and submit it — by pull request, by sending the files,
or by any other route — you agree that it may be reviewed and, if accepted,
included in Motion Graphics and distributed to everyone who uses it.

Specifically:

- **Submission is an offer, not a transfer.** Nothing happens to your template
  until it is approved. You can withdraw a submission before it is accepted.
- **Approval is not automatic.** Templates are reviewed against the contract in
  `engine/TEMPLATE_SPEC.md`. Anything may be rejected, for any reason.
- **Accepted templates ship under the same terms as the rest of the software** —
  free for everyone to use, including commercially.
- **You keep authorship.** Being included does not stop you using your own
  template however you like, anywhere else.
- **You confirm it is yours to give.** Do not submit work you do not have the
  right to license: fonts you have not licensed for embedding, copyrighted
  designs, or client work you were paid to produce exclusively.

## What the software does with your data

Nothing leaves your machine except what you explicitly ask for:

- **Renders and transcriptions run locally.** Footage and audio are never
  uploaded. The speech engine and its models are downloaded to your machine and
  run there.
- **Market data is fetched from Yahoo Finance** when you press Fetch, and only
  then. Nothing about you is sent with it.
- **Updates are checked against GitHub Releases.** That is a version number
  request; no usage data is collected.

There is no telemetry, no analytics, and no account.

## Market data and financial content

Market data is fetched from a third-party source and provided as-is. It may be
delayed, incomplete or wrong. Verify anything you publish.

The projection tool draws a path you specify. It is an illustration of an
opinion, not a forecast produced by the software, and it is deliberately styled
so viewers can tell it apart from real price history. Do not present it as data.

Nothing produced by this software is financial advice.
